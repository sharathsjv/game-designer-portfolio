About

Hi, I'm Sharath

I started at Juego Studios in Bangalore, designing levels and balancing systems for free-to-play mobile games. I also designed reward and progression systems for a financial literacy game.

At AutoVRse I turned real-world training into VR, which taught me to prototype fast and to care about how an interaction feels.

Now I build my own prototypes end to end. I write the design document first, build and playtest in Unity, and keep notes on what changes between versions.

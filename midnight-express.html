Work / Personal project

Midnight Express

You're in a line, getting closer to the body check. You're carrying contraband. If you mess up, the next smuggler you control has a harder time getting through.

Genre
Social deception
Platform
PC, keyboard or controller
Engine
Unity
Stage
Design document and Unity build, 2025
Structure

Five smugglers, one airport

A five-person smuggling group plans to move valuable goods through one airport. You guide each smuggler past security in turn, from check-in to boarding. Every stage adds one more layer of security.

Each run starts in a room. You explore to learn your ID and the restricted items for your destination, then hide the contraband through small minigames. Call a taxi from the room's phone and the airport begins.

The whole game is seen through airport security cameras. Art is PS1-style: low-poly models, 256×256 textures at most, and vertex-painted shadows.

Relay

Mistakes carry over

Whether a smuggler boards cleanly or gets caught, you move on to the next one. A clean run keeps the base alert level where it was. Getting caught raises it for the next smuggler.

Running past security works once. The next smuggler pays for it.

Every shortcut becomes a decision about the whole group, not just the current run.

Queues

Cutting the line, and fixing its balance

You can cut ahead in any queue. Each NPC you pass might refuse and send you back to the start. The more people you cut past, the higher that chance, and getting sent back raises your chance of a thorough search.

Writing this out, I saw that the risk and reward were skewed. My fix: some NPCs in the queue have open bags or pockets. Cutting puts you next to them, and you can stash your goods in their luggage, or rob it. Players who hid their goods well in the planning room can get them across without the scrutiny.

Guards

Alert levels and attention windows

Each security guard has an alert level. A calm guard has brief lapses: they look at their phone and laugh. In those windows you can cut lines unnoticed. A suspicious guard looks away for much shorter stretches.

Other travellers react too. If one spots contraband on a table, they might report you, or shake you down for part of the goods or for money.

Interview

Designed to overwhelm

At the security desk you balance a bar with the triggers while answering the officer through menus. The input switches without warning between the d-pad, both sticks and the face buttons, to create anxiety by making it hard to act smoothly. The camera zooms in as you get close to slipping.

If you're caught and run, travellers become obstacles. Bump into one and you have to recover your balance before the guards catch up.
